    Saved layouts

    Keep any placement as a separate snapshot. Restoring one first saves the layout you are leaving. Download a backup to keep it outside this browser.

    Field

    Drag to move anything except the director and the field markings. Ctrl/⌘-drag a section to rotate it; right-drag a section to resize it. Hover cables, mics and meters for details. Del removes the selected mic or meter; Esc deselects.

    Cables

    Draw a cable by hand: select a mic, snake box or the desk and drag the round plug handle beside it (or Alt-drag the item, or press W / ✎ Cable for Draw cable mode). Click the floor for bends, then click where it plugs in. Shift keeps a segment at 0/45/90°, Backspace removes the last bend, Esc cancels. Click a cable to select it: drag a square to move a bend (double-click removes it), a circle to add a bend, the diamond near the plug end to re-plug it or carry on drawing. Delete removes the selected cable's hand-drawn path. Smart route leaves hand-drawn cables alone unless "Re-route hand-drawn cables too" is ticked (Route menu).

    Windows

    Drag a window's tab or title bar onto a highlighted edge to dock it there, onto another window to add it as a tab, or anywhere else to float it. The pin button collapses a docked window to the edge; hover the edge tab to slide it out, and pin it again to dock it. The ✕ hides a window; bring it back from the Window menu.
