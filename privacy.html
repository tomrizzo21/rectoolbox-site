Privacy

Rec Toolbox · rectoolbox.net · last updated 11 November 2026

Rec Toolbox is a free, personal project for planning how to mic and record ensembles. This page explains what it keeps about you and why. In short: as little as possible, and never your recordings.

Without an account

Everything you do (layouts, gear, settings) stays in your own browser's storage on your device. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it; use File › Export to keep a copy.

With an account

If you sign in (with Google, or with an email address and password), we keep:

  • Your email address and a display name, to sign you in and to let teammates invite you.
  • What you choose to save: layouts, gear inventory, settings, and small analysis results (numbers, not audio).
  • Team membership, if you share gear with others, and any invites you send or receive.
  • Mic or interface models you submit to the shared catalog, with your account as the author.

Passwords are handled by our sign-in provider and are never visible to us. With Google sign-in we receive only your name and email address.

Recordings

Recordings are analysed in your browser. If a recording ever needs analysis on the server, it is used only for that, deleted as soon as the analysis is done, and never kept, shared or used for anything else. Only the small results are saved, and only if you choose to save them.

Who we share it with

No one, except the services that run the site: GitHub Pages (hosting) and Supabase (sign-in and database), which store data on our behalf. We don't sell data, show ads, or use tracking or advertising cookies. Sign-in uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in.

Your choices

  • Download everything we hold about you, or delete your account and all its data, from the Account menu.
  • Leave a team at any time; team owners can remove members.
  • Questions or requests: contact the site owner through the project's GitHub page.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the date above changes and signed-in users are told in the app.
